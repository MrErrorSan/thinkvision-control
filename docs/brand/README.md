# ThinkVision Control brand kit

These are instructions for designing the app's single, universal icon. They are written for a human designer, or for pasting into image generators.

## Direction

There is **one minimal mark for every platform**. It is a precise, angular-but-soft monitor drawing in **graphite and off-white**, with **one small vermilion dot** that does a job: a slider knob, a status LED or a lit pixel. The dot is a quiet nod to the TrackPoint, and the dual tone gives the restrained, engineered feel of Lenovo's Think products. It borrows only the spirit: there is no Lenovo logo, no red badge, no "Think" lettering, and the dot's colour is our own `#F05A28` rather than Lenovo red. The mark is used two ways: on a graphite tile as the app icon, and alone in one colour as the tray, menu-bar and panel glyph. The lead concept is **Knob** (the screen's bottom edge is a slider track and the dot is its knob).

## Workflow

1. **Concept.** Explore the three concepts with the prompts or by sketching. Shortlist two.
2. **Master vector.** Redraw the winner on the 48-unit grid (`app-icon.svg`) and hand-tune the 16–32 px glyph (`glyph.svg`).
3. **Exports.** Produce the Windows `.ico` files now. Produce macOS and Linux files when those ports start.
4. **Checks.** Make a contact sheet and run the review checklist.
5. **Commit.** Commit the files listed in the checklist's sign-off table.
